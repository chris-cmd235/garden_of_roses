My vow to you

A letter, kept among the roses

I once saw this message, "God gives you a partner while you're in progress. Not when you arrive at the destination. Not when life is perfect. But right in the middle of your becoming. Because the right one won't be scared of your process. They will respect it. They won't run when things get hard. They will help you grow through it. They will pray with you, build with you and remind you who you are when you forget. Most people want a finish product, but God sends people who can handle your construction phase."

I don't know why god gave me you as my partner even when you are lost in life or when you haven't figured out your emotions because I've always wondered how you will be when you have achieved everything in life or when you truly feel happy to the life you have. Well I first thought, would you still want me in your life if you ever find yourself in a position where you have everything, that only you can answer. I've arrived in your life when you fell to rock bottom. When you were still looking for yourself, scrambling the pieces that you have lost in the process of growing up. It was during the time where you were struggling with getting to know the people around you as you were forced to live a new life, where you were living (barely living at that point) with your aunt and uncle in a room so extremely hot it's almost unbearable or being to shy to eat with them kasi nahihiya ka. It was during that time where you were forced to meet new people, some people was easy to connect to, some people where the people you didn't get along with quickly but was actually one of the few people who mattered, but almost all of them served a purpose in your life. To some it gave you precious memories, sad memories, happy memories, short memories or even lets just say memories that will forever be in the past.

You've never moved on from the pain of the past as you kept coming back to it, for example memories of your childhood when you were still happy with your family, or memories of the time where all of your friends were complete and you are always with them, that to you, made your life complete and there's nothing wrong with it. We never moved on and say we no longer feel pain, we just got used to it. But there is one thing that is true, and that's what made you who you are today. Oh, that doesn't mean you are strong in any way that you no longer feel pain, no no. It just means all those memories gave you life lessons. But there is one thing that I have to clarify, you don't need to go back to the past and bear it all alone.

That's why your friends are here, and I'm here. When life gave you bitter memories it didn't forget to give you genuine connections. You were so broken when I met you that I forgot I was broken too. Because I felt it, the purpose that made my life meaningful. Then it started, I started pursuing you. For a short while it worked but since I was also still clueless I made a lot of mistakes which in turn push you away from me. Our relationship cycled through that, I was pulling you, you were pushing and we swap places, you were pulling then I was pushing again until both of us got tired. Then we fell, but my heart couldn't forget you so it was loss and wondered through time. I even thought that I may no longer be able to meet you again because it was also during that time I had the impression you transferred. But destiny played a roll, because I met you again, this time a bit more closer. Then my heart started pounding again, like a fish suddenly brought back to the sea. So I started pursuing you, this time I didn't give up when you pushed me. Because during the time that you left I've realized my only purpose in life was to fill up the life you kept missing out on. So I made it my mission, to provide you with all the things in life that life deprived you of.

Fast forward a few years, life struct me too. Our bakery started losing, online class started and my grades were unsatisfactory. I thought, what's happening to me? Because in the last few years I've been on decline. Even when I started my own business it still showed me all the limitations I can do alone. So I tried to rely on you but at that time I guess you weren't ready since you didn't know how to react or what to do, and that's fine. I made a lot of mistakes in life and I'll probably make a lot more. Now I lack a lot of things that I could have provided you with so I understand why you started to feel distant that you think I may have love you less or I was only good at the start. I get tired sometimes and made mistakes because of it. That's why I'm sorry if I failed you. I failed to make you feel the love that I've promised you for many years. That my message slipped out of my hands without thinking it through, knowing that words can make you feel loved as it can hurt.

But I want to clarify some things, I arrived in your life with no intention of leaving. The moment I met you, I know I had to be part of you. The pain in your eyes was unbearable, which is why I wanted to share it. So now I understood why I have to be part of it. I may get tired but trust me on this that I'll never leave you as everyone did. You can take your time, recover and rediscover yourself but you don't have to be afraid that you'll be alone forever, because I'll always be here waiting.

That painting represents my promise, as we may not work out now "I'll be the one that proposes in a garden of roses, and truly loves you long after our curtain closes". So rest assure baby that no one will need to fill the hole that I left, other than me. I love you very much baby, I misss you so much. Please take care of yourself baby as I can't stop myself from overthinking your well being for the rest of my life.

Chris ~always waiting

Made with love, and my unyielding fate.
